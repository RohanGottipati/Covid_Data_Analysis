Insights & Data Notes

Wave Patterns in Case Counts

All five countries show distinct waves driven by variant emergence. Delta (mid 2021) and Omicron (late 2021 to early 2022) are especially prominent. Use Cases per Million to compare across countries of very different population sizes.

Vaccination Rollout Lag

Vaccination data is effectively zero before early 2021. The rapid ramp-up from Q1 2021 onward reflects global dose procurement timelines. Canada's vaccinations_per_hundred exceeds 100% by 2023 because booster doses are counted per administration, not per person.

Scale & Normalisation

India's raw new_cases can appear much larger than others simply due to its population of ~1.38 billion. Switching to Cases per Million provides a fair cross-country comparison and is the preferred metric for epidemiological analysis.

Caveats & Limitations

Correlation between vaccination rollout and falling cases does not imply causation. Concurrent public health measures, seasonality, and variant fitness all interact. Reporting standards differ across countries, and monthly aggregation smooths over shorter term daily spikes.

Design Choices

Outlier spikes are capped (3x IQR outer fence) rather than dropped, preserving the timeline while preventing extreme values from collapsing the y axis. A 3 month rolling mean overlay is available as an optional toggle to reduce short term noise.

Missing Data Handling

Six sparse NaNs in new_vaccinations, all in pre rollout periods or isolated reporting gaps, are filled with 0. This is semantically correct for the pre rollout case and a conservative choice for later gaps, clearly flagged here rather than silently interpolated.
